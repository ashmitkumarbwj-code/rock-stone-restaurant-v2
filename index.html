Our Sacred Promise

A Sanctum of Pure Vegetarian Gastronomy

At Rock & Stone, vegetarianism is not an afterthought or an option — it is our foundational philosophy. In the Himalayan tradition of Ahimsā and wholesome mountain nutrition, every ingredient entering our kitchen is inspected for complete purity.

We strictly do not allow meat, seafood, poultry, eggs, or non-vegetarian items in our premises. Enjoy your meal with absolute spiritual peace of mind, knowing your dishes are prepared with pure mustard oil, mountain desi ghee, and fresh local produce.
