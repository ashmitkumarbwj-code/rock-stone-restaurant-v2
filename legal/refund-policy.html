Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Rock & Stone Restaurant, Kangra, HP

1. Order Cancellation Policy

Because all pure vegetarian meals are prepared fresh to order in our kitchen, orders can only be cancelled while in the PENDING status. Once an order is accepted and moved to PREPARING, cancellation is no longer possible.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are granted under the following circumstances:

  • An order was cancelled by the customer prior to kitchen acceptance.
  • The restaurant was unable to fulfill or accept the order due to ingredient availability.
  • A payment transaction was debited twice due to network disruption.

3. Refund Processing Timelines

For verified online payments, approved refunds are initiated through our payment gateway (Razorpay) to the original source account (UPI, Netbanking, or Debit/Credit Card) within 5–7 business days.
