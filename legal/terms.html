1. 100% Pure Vegetarian Covenant

Rock & Stone operates strictly as a pure vegetarian establishment. Guests are strictly prohibited from bringing outside non-vegetarian food, eggs, meat, or alcoholic beverages onto the premises.

Our restaurant opens daily at 10:00 AM and closes at 10:30 PM. The final orders for dine-in seating are accepted until 10:00 PM to ensure high-quality kitchen preparation.

Submitting a reservation request generates a PENDING booking. Tables are confirmed by management based on capacity and held for up to 15 minutes past the reserved time slot before release.
